The Game:

Your lives depend on one man's paranoia: the Tyrant. Assume the role of a tyrannical dictator and find out who among the other players is sabotaging the interests of the Committee. In this social deduction game you will test your investigative and deception skills. Will you be clever enough to save yourself before the Tyrant's paranoia reaches its peak? You will have proved your loyalty (or not) if your name does not appear among the victims of the Purges.



  • Step into the shoes of the dictator himself and carefully observe the behaviour of the players engaged in a task.
  • Gather as much information as possible in your notebook and unmask the dissidents sitting at the Committee table.
  • Keep an eye on your paranoia: when the limit is reached the purges will begin and you will be forced to decide who to execute.
  • During the purges, choose your victims carefully: leaving a dissident alive will cost you the entire game!




  • Carry out the tasks chosen by the Commissar to keep the Tyrant's paranoia at bay, so that he may have more time to track down dissidents within the Committee.
  • Use the effects of the tasks to send to the Tyrant important intelligence about the identities of other players.
  • If you have a clear conscience, all you have to do is prove to the Tyrant that you are not the one to kill.




  • Sabotage the tasks chosen by the Commissar to uncontrollably increase the Tyrant's paranoia, so that he will not have enough time to detect you and your dissident comrades.
  • Exploit the effects of the tasks to falsify the information sent to the Tyrant by the Loyalists.
